### Featured Project

**[Smart Legal Document Intelligence](https://github.com/TabarakAllahKhan/Smart_Legal_Document_Intelligence)**
A full-stack RAG platform for legal document analysis — upload a contract and get auto-generated risk summaries, clause extraction, and grounded Q&A with source citations, in English, Spanish, or Urdu.

`Express` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `pgvector` `LangChain.js` `Gemini Embeddings` `Groq / Llama 3.1` `React` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS`
